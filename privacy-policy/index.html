Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Super Pack Center ("we", "us") collects when you visit superpackcenter.com and how we use it.

Who this site is for

This site is intended for adults: parents, teachers and caregivers. It is not directed to children.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We never ask for a child's name, age or photo. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Information we collect

Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix problems.

Analytics

We use an analytics service to understand which pages are visited and how people find us. It may set cookies and collect information such as your approximate location, device type and the pages you view. We use this information in aggregate to improve the site.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to reply and to improve the site.

Cookies

You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, for example if we add analytics, advertising or a newsletter, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: insanyslandia@gmail.com.
