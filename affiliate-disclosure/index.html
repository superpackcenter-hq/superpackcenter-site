Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated October 30, 2026

At the moment, this site does not use affiliate links.

In the future we may recommend products or printables from other creators and earn a commission if you buy through our link, at no extra cost to you. If that happens, we will label each such link clearly, close to the link itself, and update this page.

Our rules for recommendations

  • We recommend only products we believe are useful for parents and teachers, and we describe them honestly.
  • Our free printables are never placed behind an affiliate link.
  • We do not copy, repost or give away paid products from other creators.
  • We do not invent reviews, discounts or deadlines.

Why we tell you this

We follow the guidance of the U.S. Federal Trade Commission on endorsements and disclosures, so you always know when we may earn money from a link.
