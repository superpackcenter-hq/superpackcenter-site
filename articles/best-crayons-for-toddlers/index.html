Best Crayons for Toddlers: Washable, Chunky and Easy to Grip

The right crayons make coloring easier for small hands and calmer for the grown-up at the table. Here is what to look for, three types of crayons that suit toddlers, and how to pair them with simple printable pages.

What to look for in toddler crayons

  • Chunky shape. Short, thick crayons are easier for a toddler to hold than thin ones.
  • Washable formula. It makes cleanup on skin, tables and walls much less stressful.
  • Non-toxic labeling. Check the box for a non-toxic statement and the manufacturer's age label.
  • Sturdy construction. Crayons that do not snap in two at the first press last longer.

Three types worth trying

1. Palm-grip crayons for first-time colorers

These are short and wide so a toddler can hold the whole crayon in a fist. They are a good first set for scribbling and mark-making. One example is the Crayola Washable Palm-Grasp Crayons (12 count), shaped to fit small hands.

Thick and easy to grip, jumbo crayons cover large areas quickly, which suits big shapes and bold outlines.

3. Washable crayons for messy days

If coloring tends to spread beyond the paper, a washable set gives you peace of mind. Always check the label to see what the formula is designed to wipe off.

Pair them with simple coloring pages

Toddlers do best with big shapes and thick lines. Try the smiling snowman in our free Christmas Friends pack, or browse our dinosaur coloring pages. Print a few copies so everyone gets a turn. If you are not sure what suits your child, our guide to choosing coloring pages by age can help, and how to print coloring pages at home shows how to save ink.

Tips for the first coloring session

  • Start with three or four colors so the choice is not overwhelming.
  • Tape the page to the table so it stays put.
  • Keep it short. Five to ten minutes is plenty.
  • Welcome the scribbles. At this age, the fun matters more than staying inside the lines.
  • Supervise young children while they color.
