About Super Pack Center

Super Pack Center is a small, independent website that publishes free printable coloring pages and activity sheets for parents, teachers and caregivers.

What you will find here

Printable PDF packs for home and classroom, sorted by topic and level, plus practical guides on printing, coloring and learning through play. Everything is free to use at home or in class.

How we make our packs

Our illustrations are created with the help of AI image tools. A person reviews every page before it is published, checking for closed outlines, clean white backgrounds and friendly, age-appropriate drawings.

Before a pack goes live we check that every page is complete, with closed outlines, a clean white background and nothing cut off, and that it prints well on US Letter paper.

Our promise on privacy

This site is for adults. We do not ask children for any personal information. Read our privacy policy for details.

How the site is funded

Our free printables stay free. In the future we may show ads or recommend products from other creators. Any paid link is labeled clearly, as explained in our affiliate disclosure.

Get in touch

Questions, requests or corrections are welcome. See our contact page.
