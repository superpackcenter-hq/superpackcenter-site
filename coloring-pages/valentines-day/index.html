Valentine's Day Coloring Pages for Kids

Hearts, love letters and Cupid, ready to print for free for a classroom party, a card-making afternoon or a cozy day at home.

Free Valentine's Day coloring pages to print

Pick a page and download it as its own PDF. Each page prints on US Letter paper, needs no sign-up and has bold outlines that are easy for little hands to color.

Ways to use them

Color the pages and use them as Valentine's cards for classmates, hang them in a window, or fold a finished love letter page into a card. For younger kids, the smiling heart is a gentle first page. The Cupid heart has a bit more to color.

Tips for Valentine's coloring

  • Print a page for every child in the class and let them color their own Valentine to give away.
  • Fold a colored page in half to make a quick card, and add a short handwritten message inside.
  • Keep crayon choices to reds, pinks and purples for a classic look, or leave all colors out for a free-for-all.
  • Younger children can focus on one big heart, while older children can add patterns and borders.

Frequently asked questions
