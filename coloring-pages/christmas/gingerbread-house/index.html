How to print it

Download the PDF, open it and print on US Letter paper at 100% scale. On A4 paper, choose Fit to page. Plain printer paper works well for crayons and markers; for watercolor, try heavier paper. More tips: how to print coloring pages at home.

Coloring ideas

  • Use bright colors for the candies and brown for the walls.
  • Add white "icing" lines with a white gel pen or crayon.
  • Pair it with a real gingerbread-decorating afternoon.

About the gingerbread house

Gingerbread houses became popular in Germany in the 1800s, and many people connect them with the fairy tale of Hansel and Gretel. Real ones are built from baked gingerbread walls held together with icing and covered with candy.

Try this activity

Give every candy on the roof its own color and pattern, then plan the house you would build if you could use any candy you like.

Best for

Toddler / Preschool. The outlines are bold and closed, so there are no tiny gaps to color around.
