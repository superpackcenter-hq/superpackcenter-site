Choosing Christmas pages by age

For toddlers and preschoolers, pick the simple pages with one big subject, such as the snowman, the tree or the gifts. Thick outlines and large open areas are easy for small hands to color.

Kindergarten and early elementary kids often enjoy the detailed winter village, which has many small areas to fill in.
