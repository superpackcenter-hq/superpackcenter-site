Free Christmas coloring pages to print

Christmas is a busy month, and a printable coloring page is one of the easiest ways to give kids a calm, creative break. Every page here is free, prints on standard US Letter paper and needs no sign-up. You can download a single picture, such as the Christmas tree, the snowman or the gingerbread house, or the whole seven-page Christmas Friends pack.

Ways to use them

Color a page each day of December as a countdown, set a stack on the table during holiday baking, or send a few home as a quiet activity for a class party. Colored pages also make cheerful cards, gift tags and window decorations. For paper and crayon ideas, see our recommendations.

Choosing Christmas pages by age

For toddlers and preschoolers, pick the simple pages with one big subject, such as the snowman, the tree or the gifts. Thick outlines and large open areas are easy for small hands to color.

Kindergarten and early elementary kids often enjoy the detailed winter village, which has many small areas to fill in.

Tips for a calm holiday coloring session

  • Print a few pages ahead and keep them in a folder, so a quiet moment is only a minute away.
  • Let children choose between two pages instead of handing out one. A small choice usually means less fuss.
  • Try colored pencils for fine details and crayons for big areas. Using both makes the finished page feel special.
  • Finished pages make good gift tags, cards or window decorations.
