Choosing dinosaur pages by age

For toddlers and preschoolers, look for thick outlines and big open areas, so crayons stay inside the lines more easily. Older kids tend to enjoy detailed scenes with small areas, such as a valley with eggs, palm trees and a volcano.

Our dinosaur packs include both kinds, so you can start with a simple page and move on to a detailed one.
