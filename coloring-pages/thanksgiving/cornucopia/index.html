How to print it

Download the PDF, open it and print on US Letter paper at 100% scale. On A4 paper, choose Fit to page. Plain printer paper works well for crayons and markers; for watercolor, try heavier paper. More tips: how to print coloring pages at home.

Coloring ideas

  • Ask kids to name each fruit while they color it.
  • Use purple for the grapes, red or green for the apples.
  • Talk about what your family is thankful for at harvest time.

About the cornucopia

The word cornucopia comes from Latin and means a horn of plenty. It comes from old Greek stories and is a symbol of a big harvest, which is why it is a favorite Thanksgiving decoration.

Try this activity

Color the fruits and vegetables in their real colors, then name each one out loud as you finish it.

Best for
