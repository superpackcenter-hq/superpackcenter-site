Choosing Thanksgiving pages by age

For toddlers and preschoolers, pick the simple pages with one big subject, such as the turkey, the pumpkin or the squirrel. Thick outlines and large open areas are easy for small hands to color.

Kindergarten and early elementary kids often enjoy the detailed harvest scene, which has many small areas to fill in.
